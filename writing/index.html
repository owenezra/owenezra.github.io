WRITING AND NOTES

The parts of AI data work that a dashboard cannot settle.

I write about the boundary between evidence and judgment: what a dataset can support, how human review should be calibrated, what automated evaluation can legitimately establish, and how data should move between owners and model teams without collapsing utility, provenance, and rights into one vague promise.
