ACCESSIBILITY

The decorative layer must never gate the content.

This site uses an animated emblem, a starfield, and constellation transitions as identity. They are built to stay out of the way of reading, navigation, and assistive technology.

Commitments

  • Semantic HTML landmarks, one main heading per page, and a logical heading order.
  • A skip link to main content on every page.
  • Keyboard-operable navigation, including the mobile menu, with visible focus states.
  • No information conveyed by hover, color, or animation alone; project statuses are written labels.
  • Text and control contrast targeted at WCAG 2.1 AA against the dark background.
  • Decorative visuals (starfield, emblem glow, constellation figures) are marked aria-hidden and receive no keyboard focus.
  • Core content remains readable when JavaScript is unavailable.

Reduced motion

When the operating system requests reduced motion (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce), the site presents a complete static design: constellation lines render fully drawn, stars and labels are visible immediately, the emblem does not float or shimmer, the starfield does not twinkle, and smooth scrolling is disabled. Nothing requires an animation to finish before it can be read or reached.

Known limitations

  • The constellation figures are decorative renderings and are hidden from assistive technology rather than described.
  • Long pages rely on in-page navigation anchors; a table of contents is not yet provided on every page.

Report an issue

If anything on this site is difficult to read, navigate, or operate with your setup, please email ahoythere@learning.voyage and describe the page, your assistive technology or input method, and the barrier. Reports are treated as defects, not feedback.
