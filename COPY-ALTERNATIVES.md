# Copy alternatives and publication choices

## 1. Client experience strip

### Named version

> Data and evaluation work supporting **Anthropic** and **Amazon** through external AI data partners.

Use only when the governing agreements permit disclosure of both organizations.

### Client-unnamed version

> Data and evaluation work supporting frontier AI and technology organizations through external AI data partners.

### Partially named version

> Data and evaluation work supporting Anthropic and other technology organizations through external AI data partners.

Use only when Anthropic may be named and the other organization may not.

## 2. Selected-work client sentence

### Named

> Selected projects have included managing data and evaluation work supporting Anthropic model development through an external AI data partner.

### Unnamed

> Selected projects have included managing data and evaluation work supporting a frontier AI lab through an external AI data partner.

## 3. AutoQA Foundation project label

### Recommended

> Public research and operating framework

This communicates substantive analysis and operational design without claiming formal publication, peer review, or standards authority.

### More conservative

> Public decision and technical framework

Use when you prefer to avoid the word “research.”

### Do not use without supporting process

- Peer-reviewed research
- Industry standard
- Certification framework
- Proven evaluation system
- Formal standard
- Independent foundation, if readers could interpret “foundation” as a legal nonprofit entity

## 4. Clarifying the word “Foundation”

If there is any risk that visitors interpret AutoQA Foundation as a nonprofit or standards body, add this line to the AutoQA footer:

> “Foundation” describes the project’s purpose as a technical and governance foundation; AutoQA Foundation is not a nonprofit organization, certification body, or standards authority.

A shorter alternative:

> AutoQA Foundation is an independent project, not a certification or standards body.

## 5. AutoQA creator attribution

### Recommended footer copy for AutoQA Foundation

> AutoQA Foundation is an independent public research and decision-support project created by Owen Onderdonk through Learning Voyage LLC. Version 0.1.0 (2026). It is not a certification body and does not establish the accuracy of any particular automated QA system.

### Less company-forward version

> AutoQA Foundation is an independent public project created by Owen Onderdonk. Version 0.1.0 (2026). It is not a certification body and does not establish the accuracy of any particular automated QA system.

### De-branded substantive pages, attributed About page

Keep the main limits, guide, and evidence pages de-branded, but add a small `About this project` link that identifies Owen as creator. This preserves the project's neutral presentation while still building a public authorship trail.

## 6. Homepage AutoQA feature

### Recommended headline

> Define the boundary before automating the verdict.

### More academic

> Capability is not authority: defining the legitimate operating region of LLM-based quality review.

### More operational

> What automated quality review may check, when it must abstain, and what must be measured before it may act.

The recommended version is the best homepage headline because it is compact, memorable, and does not overstate the project.

## 7. LinkedIn Featured descriptions

### Recommended

> A public decision and technical framework defining where LLM-assisted quality review is useful, where the record cannot support a verdict, and what must be measured before automated findings receive consequential authority. Includes capability limits, a technical guide, an evidence ledger, a source library, and a bounded pilot protocol.

### Shorter

> A public framework for the capability and authority boundaries of LLM-based quality review, with explicit abstention states, evidence requirements, calibration rules, and a bounded pilot protocol.

### Research-facing

> A public analysis and operating framework centered on record identifiability: what an automated evaluator can establish from a bounded record, what remains underdetermined, and what statistical evidence is required before a finding receives authority.

## 8. Relationship-context footer

### Named-client build

> References to named organizations describe project work supported through external AI data partners. They do not imply employment, endorsement, or a direct commercial relationship between those organizations and Learning Voyage LLC. Named references should be published only where governing agreements permit disclosure.

### Client-unnamed build

Remove the disclosure paragraph entirely or replace it with:

> Project descriptions are deliberately generalized to protect confidential client and partner information.
