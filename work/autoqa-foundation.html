PUBLIC RESEARCH & OPERATING FRAMEWORK · VERSION 0.1.0

AutoQA Foundation

Capability and authority boundaries for LLM-based quality review.

An automated evaluator can be useful without being entitled to decide. AutoQA Foundation separates those questions and turns the boundary into a system that can be inspected, measured, and governed.

THE PROBLEM

A plausible verdict is not the same as a supportable decision.

Organizations increasingly use language models to inspect human review work, code, long records, and other model outputs. The useful operating region is real: local evidence checks, deterministic consistency tests, provenance, evidence assembly, and reasoned abstention can improve review.

The failure begins when a system is treated as though more context, votes, or model capability can recover facts absent from the record, resolve instructions that do not decide the case, or justify consequences without measured error bounds.

AutoQA Foundation was created to state those limits precisely and connect them to deployment rules rather than leave them as general cautions.

CORE CONTRIBUTION

The record sets the ceiling.

If identical observable records can require different correct decisions, no evaluator that sees only those records can be correct in both cases. Better processing cannot manufacture missing facts or make an ambiguous rule decide itself.

01

Four honest outcomes

Supported, contradicted, insufficient, and contract-ambiguous remain separate. A binary pass/fail system does not escape uncertainty; it hides it.

02

Three kinds of boundary

Permanent task limits, generation-relative capability measurements, and authorization limits are treated separately so model improvement is not confused with permission to act.

03

Risk at a stated coverage

Authority depends on lane-level calibration, untouched adjudicated data, abstention behavior, and a measured false-accusation bound—not one aggregate accuracy number.

04

Human-owned consequences

Evidence can be assembled automatically; consequential interpretation remains contestable and assigned to a named human owner until the exact lane earns limited authority.

THE SYSTEM

A decision package, not a single essay.

Decision brief

A leadership-facing case for adopting defined limits of use before granting additional authority.

Capability limits

A master identifiability limit, eight derived limits, violation signatures, objections, and a worked 500,000-token review example.

Technical guide

Task-oriented guidance for evaluating a judge, designing a bounded pilot, adopting individual components, and running the reference implementation.

Evidence ledger and library

Source-level claim records, retrieved support, caveats, schemas, templates, rendered documents, and the broader research archive.

Pilot and decision controls

A shadow-mode measurement protocol, stop conditions, role ownership, and a local generator for a decision note that does not silently expand authority.

WHY IT MATTERS

One framework, three kinds of user.

Research and evaluation teams

Separates claims the record can decide from claims that require retrieval measurement, structured evidence assembly, interpretation ownership, or abstention.

Operations and quality leaders

Provides concrete release conditions, audit requirements, escalation paths, and stop decisions rather than a vague promise of model-assisted QA.

Executives and governance owners

Distinguishes capability from authority, making it possible to approve useful assistance without accidentally authorizing automated adverse action.

STATUS & LIMITATIONS

What this project does not claim.

AutoQA Foundation is an independent public research and decision-support project. It is not a certification body, a peer-reviewed standard, or evidence that any particular automated QA system is accurate.

The synthetic reference implementation demonstrates mechanics rather than real-world accuracy. Any production authority still requires instrument-specific, lane-level measurement on untouched adjudicated data and must be recalibrated after material configuration changes.

The project is intentionally de-branded so the operating boundary can be evaluated independently of any employer, vendor, or proposed deployment.
